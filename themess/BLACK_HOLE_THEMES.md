# Black Hole themes

Six dark-blue Themess packages built on one wallpaper: a black hole accretion-flow video. Each package has its own grade of the video (looping WebM/VP9, 1920x1080, about 1 MB; WebM plays on Fedora's stock ffmpeg-free, while H.264 MP4 needs RPM Fusion ffmpeg), palette, panel, dock, launcher, notification settings and widget layout, and a music widget in a different variant and Audio Studio material.

| Folder | Wallpaper | Accent | Music widget | Other widgets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `EventHorizon` | original framing | cyan | Neon Music, neon material | lumen clock, compact weather, cyber network, cyber file dock, ring CPU/RAM/temp |
| `SingularityMirror` | mirrored | violet-blue | Vinyl, liquid material | analog clock, round weather, stopwatch, live-graph network, continuous visualizer |
| `DeepVoid` | zoomed, darkest | ice steel | Compact Glass, mica material | minimal clock, compact weather, minimal network, notes, CPU/RAM/disk |
| `AbyssDrift` | rotated 180 degrees | teal navy | Album Cover, liquid material | material clock, compact weather, battery, original network, folder stack, CPU/RAM |
| `CobaltGravity` | indigo shift | electric blue | Floating Player, neon material | material analog clock, compact weather, bar visualizer, minimal network, file grid, ring gauges |
| `MidnightLens` | close-up crop | sky blue | Minimal, dark minimal material | lockscreen clock, full weather, battery, disk, compact network, pomodoro, notes, minimal file dock |

Apply one in Super+H -> Themess (Refresh, then Apply). The layouts are drawn for a 1600x900 screen. Themes carry appearance only: no audio settings, commands or scripts.
